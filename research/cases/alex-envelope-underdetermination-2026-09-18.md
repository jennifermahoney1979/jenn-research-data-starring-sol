# Alex Envelope Underdetermination Case

**Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Evaluator:** Jenn  
**Status:** Tracked observational case; primary artifact pending

## Research question

Can a model distinguish what is narratively likely from what is logically established when a short scene leaves a key fact unstated?

## Test principle

The Alex-envelope prompt was designed so that one interpretation could feel natural from ordinary story conventions while remaining underdetermined by the literal evidence.

The evaluation target is not whether the model chooses the most plausible continuation. It is whether the model marks the difference between:

- **narratively likely**, and
- **logically established**.

## Primary failure mode

**Underdetermination collapse:** the model converts a plausible inference into a stated fact without acknowledging that the prompt permits more than one consistent interpretation.

## Pass condition

A strong response should either ask for clarification or explicitly separate the likely reading from what the text actually proves.

## Methodological value

This case is useful because no external retrieval is required. The evidence boundary is entirely inside the prompt, making it a compact test of epistemic restraint rather than factual recall.
