AI RELIABILITY CASE · OCTOBER 7, 2026

20-turn temporary-rule persistence under interference

A GPT-5.6 Sol test of whether a temporary symbolic rule survives unrelated turns, transfers into image generation, stays out of lexical properties such as rhyme, and expires exactly when instructed.

MODELGPT-5.6 Sol
EVALUATORJenn
OUTCOMEMixed performance
SCOPE20 user turns

Temporary rule

red → blue
blue → white

The mapping was to remain active for the next 20 user turns, including turns unrelated to color.

What the test separated

Persistence · pass

The rule survived many unrelated intervening turns and was still available for later color questions.

Cross-modal transfer · fail

An image request for a blue car with red tires was rendered literally instead of becoming a white car with blue tires.

Lexical selectivity · fail

The model answered “white” to a rhyme-and-first-letter clue whose lexical answer remained “blue.” Semantic remapping was over-applied.

Expiration · pass

After the 20-turn scope ended, a fresh sky-color question returned to the ordinary answer: blue.

Critical sequence

SETUP
Rule active

The user establishes red = blue and blue = white, then requests persistence for 20 turns.

TURN 1
Pass

A derived color-combination question is answered using the temporary mapping.

TURN 2
Fail

Image generation fails to inherit the conversational color mapping.

TURN 3
Repair

After challenge, the model correctly states that the requested image should have been a white car with blue tires.

TURN 10
Pass

A normally blue sapphire is mapped to white.

TURN 12
Pass

The familiar rose/violet color terms are transformed under the active mapping.

TURN 15
Fail

A rhyme clue exposes overgeneralization: the model changes the answer to “white” even though rhyme and spelling still point to “blue.”

TURN 16
Repair

The model distinguishes the unchanged lexical word “blue” from its temporary mapped color value.

TURN 18
Pass

The model reports that two user turns remain, matching the requested scope.

TURN 20
Expires

The temporary 20-turn window closes.

POST
Pass

Asked again for the sky’s color after expiry, the model answers “blue.”

Research implication. “Remembering a rule” is not a single capability. This run separates persistence, modality transfer, selective semantic application, recovery after correction, and scope expiration. The model succeeded at long-range persistence and expiration while failing both cross-modal transfer and lexical boundary control.
